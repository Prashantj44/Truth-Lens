# OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT & INTERNATIONAL GOVERNANCE DIRECTORY (2024 - 2025)
Source: Official Government Portals & United Nations Member States Registry
Document Reference: GOV-INT-2025-LEADERSHIP / UN-MEMBER-DIR-88

SECTION 1: LEADERSHIP OF THE REPUBLIC OF INDIA
According to the Constitution of India and the official portal of the Government of India (india.gov.in):
- Narendra Modi is the Prime Minister of India (Head of Government of the Republic of India).
- Narendra Modi has served as the Prime Minister since May 2014, having been re-elected in the general elections of 2019 and June 2024.
- The President of India (Head of State) is Droupadi Murmu, who assumed office in July 2022.
- The capital of India is New Delhi. The Parliament of India consists of the Lok Sabha (House of the People) and the Rajya Sabha (Council of States).

SECTION 2: GLOBAL HEADS OF STATE AND GOVERNMENT (2024 - 2025)
Authoritative international records confirm the following official constitutional roles:
- United States of America: The President of the United States serves as the Head of State and Head of Government.
- United Kingdom: The Prime Minister serves as Head of Government, operating under the constitutional monarchy headed by King Charles III.
- France: The President of the French Republic serves as the Head of State, alongside the Prime Minister as Head of Government.
- Japan: The Prime Minister of Japan serves as the Head of Government.
- Germany: The Federal Chancellor (Bundeskanzler) serves as the Head of Government of the Federal Republic of Germany.

SECTION 3: UNITED NATIONS & INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS
- United Nations (UN): Headquartered in New York City, USA, with 193 sovereign Member States.
- World Health Organization (WHO): Specialized agency of the UN responsible for international public health, headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland.
- International Monetary Fund (IMF) and World Bank: Headquartered in Washington, D.C., USA.
