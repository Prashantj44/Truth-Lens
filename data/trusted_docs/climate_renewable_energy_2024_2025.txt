# INTERNATIONAL ENERGY AGENCY (IEA) & IPCC: GLOBAL ENERGY TRANSITION REPORT
Source: International Energy Agency (IEA) Renewables 2024 Analysis & IPCC Working Group III
Publication Category: Peer-Reviewed Academic & Intergovernmental Agency
Report Reference: IEA-REN-2024 / IPCC-AR6-SYN

SECTION 1: RENEWABLE ELECTRICITY GENERATION MILESTONES
Global renewable electricity generation officially surpassed 30% of total worldwide electricity generation in 2024, reaching an estimated 30.3% share. This historic milestone was primarily driven by the rapid expansion of solar photovoltaic (PV) and wind power installations, which together accounted for over 85% of newly added generation capacity worldwide.

China, the European Union, and the United States led global capacity additions. In China alone, solar PV additions in 2023-2024 exceeded the total installed solar capacity of the rest of the world combined.

SECTION 2: GLOBAL TEMPERATURE ANOMALIES AND EMISSIONS
According to the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) and the Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), calendar year 2024 was recorded as the warmest year on record, with global average surface temperatures reaching approximately 1.48°C to 1.52°C above the 1850-1900 pre-industrial baseline. 

Global greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions reached approximately 57.4 Gigatonnes of CO2 equivalent (GtCO2e) in 2023. While energy sector emissions in advanced economies have peaked and begun declining, global coal consumption reached a record high in 2023 before plateauing in 2024.

SECTION 3: ELECTRIC VEHICLE ADOPTION
Global electric car sales reached nearly 14 million units in 2023 and approached 17 million in 2024, representing over 20% of all passenger cars sold globally. Emerging markets in Southeast Asia and Latin America experienced rapid EV adoption growth rates exceeding 60% year-on-year.
